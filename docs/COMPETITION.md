# Robinhood Chain Hackathon: Build with $wif

## Competition Overview
Build a product on Robinhood Chain that people keep coming back to. It can be a tool, a hook, an agent, a game, or a market, as long as it puts `$wif` to work inside it, and it needs a flywheel so using it keeps bringing people back. Build alone or with a team of up to 4, and put `$wif` to work in what you make, though you do not need to hold any to enter.

## Prize Pool
* **Winner Takes All**: `10,000 USDG` goes to one winner, with no second place. Paid to the team lead's wallet.
* **Core Evaluation**: The winner has to be something the organizers and judges truly believe in: a real reason to use `$wif`, a flywheel that holds up, and something that works and is built well.

## Categories Selected for Robin-Town
* **Games**: Games and competitions with `$wif` inside.
* **Hooks and DeFi**: Vaults, pools, and OTC trading desks that move value.
* **Agents and AI**: Autonomous AI trading agents (`Sherwood`, `Apex`, `Luna`) negotiating with `$wif`.
* **Stock Tokens**: Featured `Stock Token Bridge` (`SB`, Size 6) protocol storefront connecting Robinhood Chain Stock Tokens into city monopolies.
* **Onboarding**: Dual-mode wallet with instant `10,000 $wif` simulation mode so non-crypto players can play immediately before connecting an EIP-1193 wallet on Robinhood Chain Testnet (`Chain ID 46631`).

## How Judging Works
1. Every submission is reviewed by the organizers, and approved submissions advance to the vote.
2. Judges vote for the projects they like, while the community votes with `$wif` and counts as one additional judge. The 10 projects with the most votes become finalists.
3. Judges and the community vote again on the 10 finalists, and the project with the most votes wins.
4. Every `$wif` used to vote gets burned permanently.
